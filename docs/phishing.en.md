# Phishing and scams

## What is phishing?

It is a **trick**: someone pretends to be a trusted company (your bank, the post office, an online shop...) so you hand over passwords, card numbers or other data. It arrives by **email, SMS, WhatsApp or phone call**.

!!! info "Fun fact"
    Fake messages always try the same thing: to make you **act fast without thinking**.

## Warning signs

1. **Urgency**: "Your account will be blocked in 24 hours".
2. **Unexpected prizes**: "You have won a phone".
3. **Spelling** or translation mistakes.
4. **Strange sender**: the email comes from an address that isn't the official one.
5. **Suspicious links**: the text says one thing, but the real address is another.
6. **They ask for data the bank never asks for**: passwords, PINs or full codes.

## Example of a fake message

!!! failure "Suspicious message"
    **From:** soporte@banc0-seguro-info.com

    *Dear customer: we detected strange activity on your account. Click here to verify your identity within 2 hours or your account will be blocked.*

??? question "Why is it fake?"
    - Odd address (`banc0` with a zero, a domain that isn't the bank's).
    - It rushes you with a 2-hour deadline.
    - It asks you to click a link instead of going to the official website yourself.
    - It doesn't use your real name.

## What to do if you get a suspicious message

1. **Don't click** any link or open attachments.
2. **Don't reply** or call numbers that appear in the message.
3. If in doubt, go to the official website **yourself** by typing the address, or call the number on your card.
4. **Delete** the message (and report it as spam or phishing).

- [ ] I know my bank will never ask for my password by message
- [ ] I don't click links in unexpected messages
- [ ] I check the sender before trusting

## What if I already fell for it?

!!! danger "Act fast"
    1. **Change the password** of that account (from a trusted device).
    2. If you gave bank details, **call the bank** and block the card.
    3. Turn on [two-step verification](contrasenas.md).
    4. Check your account movements.
    5. If you lost money, **report it** to the police.

## How to see where a link really goes

Without clicking it, **hover the mouse over it** (on a computer) or **press and hold** (on a phone). The real address will appear at the bottom. If it doesn't match the company's, it's fake.

```text
Link text:       www.mybank.com/customers
Real address:    http://mybank.customers-verify.xyz   <-- FAKE!
```
