# Conclusions

## What we have learned

Protecting a device **doesn't require being an expert**, just repeating a few simple habits:

1. Use **strong and different passwords**, with two-step verification.
2. Keep everything **up to date**.
3. Keep **antivirus protection** on.
4. Make **backups**.
5. **Distrust** urgent and unexpected messages.
6. Take care of the **Wi-Fi** we use.

## Final checklist

Tick your state again. Compare it with the one you noted in [Before you start](requisitos.md).

- [ ] My passwords are different and long
- [ ] I have two-step verification on email and bank
- [ ] My system and apps are up to date
- [ ] My antivirus is on
- [ ] I have automatic backups
- [ ] I can identify phishing
- [ ] My home Wi-Fi is well protected

!!! success "If you ticked almost everything"
    Congratulations! Your device is better protected than most people's.
